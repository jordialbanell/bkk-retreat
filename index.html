Dress

What to wear.

  • Friday

    One outfit all night, smart casual. Tichuca and Sing Sing both enforce it and Sing Sing is strict: collared shirt or smart tee, chinos or smart trousers, proper shoes. No shorts, flip-flops or sportswear. Bring your passport for Tichuca's ID check.

  • Saturday daytime

    Comfy, light clothes. You'll sweat over woks, wear something you don't mind getting splashed. Closed shoes are safer in the kitchen.

  • Saturday night

    Smart casual again, can go a notch up. Aether is a 44th-floor cocktail lounge and Pastel turns into a party. Same outfit works for Havana and Baccarat. No shorts or flip-flops.

  • Sunday

    Casual. For temples, covered shoulders and knees are required at the Grand Palace and Wat Pho.

Only Tichuca and Sing Sing have a confirmed dress code, the rest are recommendations.
